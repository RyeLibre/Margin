MARGIN - read, mark up and listen to PDF and EPUB books (Windows)
==================================================================

Install
  1. Unzip this folder (right-click the zip > Extract All).
  2. Double-click "Install Margin".
     If Windows says "Windows protected your PC", click "More info" > "Run anyway".
     (Margin is a small home-made app, so it isn't signed by a big publisher.)
  3. Margin opens, and there's a Margin shortcut on your Desktop and in the Start menu.

Getting started
  - Press "Open" (or drag a PDF or EPUB onto the window). Books stay on your PC.
  - Library > "Add a folder": Margin brings in every PDF and EPUB in that folder
    and checks it for new ones each time it starts (the files are never copied).
  - Highlight: select text and pick a colour. "Note" adds a note to it.
  - Save word: select or double-click a word > "Save word". Review them as
    flashcards in the Words tab.
  - Listen: reads aloud from where you are, with the spoken word underlined.
    The natural "neural" voice downloads once (about 90 MB, needs internet the
    first time); after that everything works offline.
  - Minimize Margin while it reads and a small pause button stays on top of your
    other windows. The taskbar preview and your keyboard's play/pause key work too.
  - Library > "Back up library" saves all books, notes and words in one file.

Everything stays on your PC. To remove Margin, double-click "Uninstall Margin".
Needs Windows 10 or 11 (with the Microsoft Edge WebView2 Runtime, which almost all PCs have).
